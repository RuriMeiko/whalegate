**The dashboard shows which version is running.** The version sits next to the title and links to its release notes.

## What changed

- The running version (for example `v0.5.22`) appears beside "whalegate/rust" at the top of the dashboard. Click it to open the release notes for that version.
- Fixed a doubled word in the banner that appears when DeepSeek logs out several accounts at once.

## Upgrade

From the dashboard, or re-run the installer:

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RuriMeiko/whalegate/main/install.sh | sh

    irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RuriMeiko/whalegate/main/install.ps1 | iex
