**Images that DeepSeek briefly refuses no longer sink the request, and a DNS hiccup no longer counts against your accounts.** A DeepSeek audit failure (`err=50404`) was burning all three retries on three different accounts, and a flaky DNS lookup on your own machine was marking healthy accounts as failing.

## Upgrade

From the dashboard, or re-run the installer:

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RuriMeiko/whalegate/main/install.sh | sh

    irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RuriMeiko/whalegate/main/install.ps1 | iex

## Fixed

- **Image upload retries on the same account first.** DeepSeek sometimes fails its content audit with `status=FAILED err=50404` (marked `retryable: true`) on an image that uploads fine a moment later: the same screenshot passed 12 of 12 re-uploads across four accounts. An upload that gets this is now repeated up to two more times on the same account before the request moves to another one.
- **Accounts only die when every attempt is refused.** If the image is still rejected after all retries, the accounts involved are taken out of the pool for 30 minutes with a clear label. A single refusal no longer penalizes the account, and a successful attempt in between leaves them alone.
- **DNS and connection errors don't count against accounts.** A `dns error ... Try again` or connect failure reaching DeepSeek is local, not the account's fault. It no longer raises the account's failure streak, and it grants up to three extra attempts so a short network blip doesn't fail the request.

## Notes

- `cargo test`: 87 passed, including a new test that DNS and connect errors are recognized as network errors while auth errors are not.
- The cause of the 50404 itself is still unknown; it didn't reproduce on a direct upload, so this release works around it rather than removing it.
