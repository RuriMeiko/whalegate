**Request logging no longer blocks request handling.** The per-request trace (`trace.jsonl`) and the saved samples of unparsed replies (`samples.jsonl`) were written to disk inside request handling. They now go through a single background writer, which keeps lines in order and rotates `trace.jsonl` at 20 MB as before.

## Upgrade

From the dashboard, or re-run the installer:

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RuriMeiko/whalegate/main/install.sh | sh

    irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RuriMeiko/whalegate/main/install.ps1 | iex
