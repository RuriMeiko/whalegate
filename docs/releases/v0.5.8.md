**A few logged-out accounts no longer fail a request while the pool still has working ones.** DeepSeek revoked three accounts' tokens at the same time. The next request hit all three in a row, each failing instantly with `AUTH_INVALID: Authorization Failed (invalid token)`, and whalegate gave up after its three attempts, even though four healthy accounts were idle.

## Upgrade

From the dashboard, or re-run the installer:

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RuriMeiko/whalegate/main/install.sh | sh

    irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RuriMeiko/whalegate/main/install.ps1 | iex

## Fixed

- **A rejected token does not use up a retry.** When DeepSeek rejects an account's token, whalegate marks that account "invalid token — cần login lại" as before, takes it out of rotation, and moves on to another account without counting the try. The three attempts are kept for real failures: empty streams, cut-off replies and unparseable tool calls. Up to eight dead accounts are skipped per request.

## Notes

- Accounts whose token was rejected still need to be signed in again from the dashboard. Until then they stay out of rotation.
