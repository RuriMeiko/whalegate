**Clearer error when every account is busy.** When a request ran out of accounts during failover, the real reason (pool exhausted, accounts cooling down) was dropped and the client only saw a generic "hết acc khả dụng". The actual message now reaches the client.

## Upgrade

From the dashboard, or re-run the installer:

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RuriMeiko/whalegate/main/install.sh | sh

    irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RuriMeiko/whalegate/main/install.ps1 | iex
