# v0.5.32

**Muted accounts are recognised.**

- When DeepSeek answers a completion with `biz_code 5 "user is muted"` (`mute_until`), the account now rests **until exactly that time** and the dashboard says why ("DeepSeek muted this account until …"). Before, it was treated as a generic error: the fail streak grew and the account was retried every few minutes. A mute does not count as a failure, does not disable the account, and is skipped like a logged-out account while another one can answer.
- Status texts for muted and rate-limited accounts are translated (en / 中文).
