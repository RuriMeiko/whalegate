**Web search no longer gives up early when the pool is busy.** It now waits for a free account for its whole 45s budget (was 10s), and the error names the real cause (e.g. pool exhausted) instead of a generic "no account available".

## Upgrade

From the dashboard, or re-run the installer:

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RuriMeiko/whalegate/main/install.sh | sh

    irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RuriMeiko/whalegate/main/install.ps1 | iex
