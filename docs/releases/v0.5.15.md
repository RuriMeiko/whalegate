**Tool calls with mangled DSML markers are recognised again.** The model sometimes writes the bars as `__DSML__` (`<｜__DSML__｜__DSML__｜ parameter ...>`) or as `∣`. Those blocks used to leak to the client as plain text, or get cut off inside the code, with no retry. They are now parsed like normal tool calls.

## Upgrade

From the dashboard, or re-run the installer:

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RuriMeiko/whalegate/main/install.sh | sh

    irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RuriMeiko/whalegate/main/install.ps1 | iex
